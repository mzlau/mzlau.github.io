I programmed, customized and diagnosed a scaled connected automated vehicle (CAV) platform running over a pLTE network.

- Evaluated collective control of several CAVs together with lower-level chassis control in real-world experiments.
- Used the platform to move algorithms from simulation to hardware and to find where they break.
