As aerodynamic lead of Cyclone Racing at Iowa State University (2014 to 2018), I led the design of the aero packages for three Formula Student cars.

- Front and rear wings, active aerodynamics, nosecone and sidepods.
- CFD and FEA, run on high-performance computing, to optimize the designs; a drag reduction system cut peak longitudinal drag force by 61%.
- Built the composite parts, assembled the packages and supported field tests and competitions.
